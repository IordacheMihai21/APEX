# SEO, share mechanics and retention for daily web games (context: Lapdle)

Research date: 2026-11-11. Evidence quality varies a lot. Ahrefs/Semrush/Similarweb figures are modeled estimates. Many "case study" pages about Duolingo and Wordle are secondary or AI-written. Each item is labeled where it is weak.

## 1. SEO for daily games: which queries bring traffic and how dle sites rank

### Takeaway
Most daily-game search demand goes to (a) brand or brand+modifier queries ("<game> unlimited", "<game> hint today") and (b) recurring "today's answer/hint" queries that publishers farm every day. Generic "daily <topic> game" demand is small next to these. A niche dle wins search through brand-adjacent naming, an unlimited/practice mode, a fresh daily page and topical "vs"/"best X games" content. Lapdle's niche ("F1 wordle", "F1 daily game") is already crowded with content-marketing competitors.

### Cited Findings
- "wordle unlimited" is a very large modifier query. Ahrefs (Oct 2024 snapshot) lists about 1.6M US monthly volume, with wordleunlimited.org ranking #1 and getting about 598K visits/month from that keyword alone. wordleunlimited.io ranks #3 with about 193K visits from it (volume shown there about 1.7M) — [Ahrefs: wordleunlimited.org](https://ahrefs.com/ja/websites/wordleunlimited.org); [ahrefstop: wordleunlimited.io](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/wordleunlimited.io)
- Other dle brand queries in the same Ahrefs data: quordle about 3.5M, feudle about 46K, victordle about 29K monthly searches (undated) — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/ja/websites/wordleunlimited.org)
- Semrush's figures for "wordle unlimited" range from about 35K to several hundred thousand depending on country database. Tool estimates are not consistent with each other — [Semrush wordleunlimited.org competitors](https://www.semrush.com/website/wordleunlimited.org/competitors)
- Similarweb's top keywords for a clone site (wordle-game.co) are other clone names: kilowordle, countryle, tridle, kilordle, phrasle. Clone sites rank on the names of other dle games. Traffic was falling about 39% — [Similarweb wordle-game.co](https://www.similarweb.com/website/wordle-game.co/)
- Naming a game "-ordle"/"-dle" is described as a way to surface for people searching "Wordle" (commentary, not measured) — [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/wordle-copies-online-games-business/)
- "Today's answer/hint" pages are a large, predictable SEO play. Players search the same terms at the same time each day, and SEO-savvy publishers (Mashable, Forbes, RockPaperShotgun and others) rank and monetize with display ads — [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/who-profits-wordle-hint-sites-inside-billionclick-business-solutions-today-1754238); [Machined Substack (critical opinion)](https://machined.substack.com/p/todays-wordle-answer-is-everything)
- "mashable connections hint" alone is about 21K monthly searches. "Freshness is not optional for daily puzzle searches; it is part of the search intent" — [Ranktracker](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/mashable-connections-hint-today/)
- A small hint site, wordlehints.co.uk, gets about 35K UK visits/month per Ahrefs, so even niche hint sites capture steady traffic — [ahrefstop](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/wordlehints.co.uk)
- Search interest in the original game decays. Semrush estimates wordle.com (not the NYT game URL) at about 36K visits in Feb 2026, and the Substack notes "Wordle" search volume trailing off after the peak — [Semrush wordle.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/wordle.com/overview/); [Machined](https://machined.substack.com/p/todays-wordle-answer-is-everything)
- The F1 daily-game SERP is crowded: Paddockdle, Driverle and other formula1points.com games (one play per mode per day), Formuladle (iOS), FormuDle Grid, Lights Out and Sportsdle. Sportsdle publishes many SEO pages ("Best F1 Wordle Games", "Stewardle vs Sportsdle", "Paddockdle vs Sportsdle", "F1 Wordle Alternatives") and offers an unlimited mode, grid and season calendar. I found no independent popularity data — [Sportsdle best F1 wordle games](https://sportsdle.com/blog/best-f1-wordle-games); [Sportsdle vs Paddockdle](https://sportsdle.com/blog/paddockdle-vs-sportsdle); [Driverle](https://www.formula1points.com/what-is-driverle); [10015.io Paddockdle](https://10015.io/product/paddockdle)
- Directories and cross-promotion are a distribution channel for daily games. Relaydle lists 318 daily games ranked by plays (weekly half-life). One case study cites 1,690 cross-promo "rail" redirects versus 6 directory clicks over Sept 1-28, 2026 for one game (Le Bon Rang) — [Relaydle](https://relaydle.com/)
- WTFSEO built "Searchdle", a niche-topic Wordle clone made explicitly for ad revenue/SEO. No results published — [WTFSEO](https://wtfseo.com/introducing-searchdle-the-seo-version-of-wordle/)

### Inferences
- For Lapdle, the reachable queries are probably "F1 wordle", "F1 dle", "F1 daily game", "F1 game unlimited", "<circuit> racing line" and "guess the F1 track/circuit". Competitors already target "best F1 wordle games" and "X vs Y" pages, so Lapdle could publish its own comparison/alternatives page and get listed on others' lists and on directories like Relaydle.
- An "unlimited"/practice mode (past circuits, random) has strong precedent as an SEO landing page. The archive of past daily puzzles doubles as programmatic pages ("Lapdle #123 — Monza").
- Event-driven SEO (race-week circuit pages timed to the F1 calendar) fits the freshness intent seen in hint queries. I found no measured case study for race-week pages specifically.
- Lapdle's daily answer is unique to Lapdle, so no third party will write "today's Lapdle answer" pages until it is large. Its own spoiler-guarded hint page could capture that intent later.

### Gaps
- No creator-published Search Console case study with numbers for a niche dle site was found.
- No reliable volume for generic "daily <topic> game" or "<topic> dle" queries, and none for F1-specific keywords. This needs a direct check in Ahrefs/Semrush/GSC.
- No evidence was found for or against Google penalizing programmatic archive pages on dle sites.

## 2. Share mechanics: what makes results spread

### Takeaway
Wordle's emoji grid worked because it was spoiler-free (it shows the guess pattern, not the answer), comparable (everyone had the same puzzle that day), compact and text-only (pastes anywhere) and curiosity-inducing. It began as a user behaviour that Wardle then productized. Measured share rates for indie daily games are low single digits to mid-teens per win.

### Cited Findings
- The colored-tile format was invented by players. In late 2021 a New Zealand Twitter group (#DailyWordleClub) adopted emoji squares because they were easier to share and spoiler-free, and Wardle later added it to the share button. The group's norm was never to post the answer, which kept it welcoming to newcomers — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/stefficao/how-wordle-went-viral-strategy)
- Growth timeline: about 90 daily users at the Nov 2021 release, about 2M daily by Jan 2022. About 1.2M tweets contained the square emojis Jan 1-13, 2022. Searches spiked first in NZ (mid-Dec), then in Australia, the UK and Canada after Guardian Australia (Dec 23) and the NYT profile (Jan 3, 2022) — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/stefficao/how-wordle-went-viral-strategy)
- The share button was added around Dec 16, 2021. A low-reliability timeline cites about 12K daily players in Dec 2021 rising to about 300K on Jan 1, 2022 (treat with caution) — [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/wordle-online-word-game); [dinogame.gg (low reliability)](https://dinogame.gg/blog/why-wordle-blew-up/)
- NYT bought Wordle in Jan 2022 for a "low seven-figure sum" — [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/toronto/wordle-online-word-game)
- Measured share rates. One author running five daily games reports share rates per win from 3.3% to 16.4%. DailyFoot was highest at 16.4% of wins ending in a share (self-reported, undated) — [Relaydle](https://relaydle.com/)
- Media amplification mattered. Journalists who played wrote about it (228 articles in under a month) — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/stefficao/how-wordle-went-viral-strategy)
- NYT itself frames shared moments as news: 5.6M Wordle streaks broke on a single hard puzzle ("corer", Oct 15, 2024), and 2.8M people used the same starting word every day. Shared difficulty creates talkable moments — [TODAY via AOL](https://www.aol.com/exclusive-wordle-puzzle-ended-5-013624721.html)

### Inferences
- For Lapdle, the share result should be spoiler-free (no circuit name or line shown), include the puzzle number/date (so everyone compares the same puzzle), be paste-able as plain text/emoji (works in WhatsApp/X/Discord where image cards are friction) and stay short. An image share card is a complement, not a replacement.
- Share rate per completed game is the KPI to instrument (Relaydle's 3-16% range is the only benchmark found). At 10-20 DAU, even 16% sharing gives about 2-3 shares/day, so share-driven growth will be slow until DAU rises. Seeding into existing F1 communities (subreddits, Discords) mirrors the NZ group-chat origin.
- Hard days are talkable. A daily "difficulty/average score" line in the share text gives people something to compare.

### Gaps
- No published data on challenge-a-friend links or referral rewards (such as cosmetic unlocks) for free web games. Lapdle's colour-reward invites are untested against any benchmark.
- No data comparing image cards vs text emoji share conversion.
- No measured click-through/install rate from a shared result back to the game.

## 3. Retention: streaks, notifications, leaderboards, archives, scarcity; benchmarks

### Takeaway
Streaks have the strongest evidence (Duolingo ran 600+ streak experiments; first 7 days are the fragile window; flexibility features like freezes/amulets give measurable lifts of a few percent). Web push opt-in is low by default (about 12% desktop / 23% Android grant rates in Chrome telemetry) and much better when asked contextually after value is shown. Mobile-game retention medians (about 22% D1 / 4% D7 / 0.7-0.85% D30) are the closest public benchmark, and puzzle sits at the top of casual for D7. No public D1/D7/D30 benchmark for web daily games was found.

### Cited Findings
**Streaks**
- Duolingo ran 600+ streak-related experiments. Retention is "most fragile in the first seven days" (Jackson Shuttleworth, Group PM Retention, Dec 2024) — [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/behind-the-product-duolingo-streaks)
- Duolingo's own blog: learners offered the Weekend Amulet were 4% more likely to come back a week later and 5% less likely to lose their streak. Streak Wager (keep a streak for 7 more days) increased return frequency and lessons completed, with no % in the excerpt — [Duolingo blog: how streaks keep learners committed](https://making.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals)
- Streak freezes are pre-equipped insurance for a missed day. Duolingo also auto-repairs streaks broken by outages — [Duolingo blog: protecting streaks](https://blog.duolingo.com/protecting-streaks-from-site-issues)
- Secondary/unverified: a streak animation raised D7 for new learners by 1.7%. Learners reaching a 7-day streak are "3.6x more likely to complete their course". Freeze reduced churn 21% for at-risk users. None of these was traced to a primary source — [jtbdux-protocol](https://jtbdux-protocol.pages.dev/adoption-and-behavior/behavior-design-patterns/how-duolingo-engineered-50-million-daily-users-and-the-backlash-that-came-with-it); [blitzllama](https://www.blitzllama.com/blog/duolingo-product-management-lessons)
- Streak loss is a mass event. 5.6M Wordle streaks ended on one puzzle in 2024 — [TODAY via AOL](https://www.aol.com/exclusive-wordle-puzzle-ended-5-013624721.html)
- Players look up hints specifically to protect streaks (Connections), so streak pressure drives hint-search SEO demand (low-quality aggregator, qualitative only) — [search results, aggregator pages; treat as weak]

**Daily habit at scale (NYT)**
- NYT puzzles were played about 11.1B times in 2024: Wordle 5.3B, Connections 3.3B, Mini 2.1B, Strands 1.3B — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91386818/pips-new-game-nyt-the-next-wordle); [TODAY via AOL](https://www.aol.com/exclusive-wordle-puzzle-ended-5-013624721.html)
- In 2025 total plays held at about 11.2B but Wordle fell to about 4.2B. NYT wants a Wordle TV show to "re-energize" lapsed players, so even the best daily game decays and needs new formats — [A Media Operator](https://www.amediaoperator.com/analysis/wordle-tv-show-new-york-times-games-jonathan-knight/)
- Games are cited as central to NYT's subscription strategy — [Axios, Jan 2024](https://www.axios.com/2024/01/29/wordle-nyt-games-news-media-layoffs)

**Web push**
- Chrome telemetry: notifications are 74% of all permission prompts and the least granted, at 12% grant rate on desktop and 23% on Android. A third-party CrUX analysis estimated an average of 17% accept, 41% reject, 24% dismiss and 19% ignore, with phones accepting more than desktop — [Chromium blog: quieter permission UI (Jan 2020)](https://blog.chromium.org/2020/01/introducing-quieter-permission-ui-for.html); [Chrome dev blog: notification permission data in CrUX](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/notification-permission-data-in-crux/); [DEV / Chromium devs "Yestifications"](https://dev.to/chromiumdev/yestifications-exploring-how-users-engage-with-notification-prompts-in-the-chrome-ux-report-4h7c)
- Sites with very low acceptance are auto-enrolled in Chrome's quiet prompt UI. Asking on first arrival gives very low accept rates. Chrome recommends in-page pre-prompts or asking in context — [Chromium blog](https://blog.chromium.org/2020/01/introducing-quieter-permission-ui-for.html); [Google Research "Shhh...be quiet!"](https://research.google/pubs/pub49767/)
- Vendor web push benchmarks (directional): opt-in 5-15% of visitors (2019 survey); merchants about 2.7% opt-in; web push CTR about 3.68% (range 2.3-4%) — [MobiLoud](https://mobiloud.com/blog/push-notification-statistics); [PushOwl docs](https://docs.pushowl.com/en/articles/6361107-web-push-notification-benchmarks). App push Android CTR averaged 2.25% (Pushwoosh, Q4 2024-Q2 2025) — [Pushwoosh](https://www.pushwoosh.com/blog/push-notification-benchmarks/)

**Retention benchmarks (mobile, closest available)**
- GameAnalytics 2026 report (16,262 games): median D1 about 22%, D7 about 4%, D30 about 0.7%. Top 25% just over 30% D1, 6-7% D7, about 1.6-1.8% D30. Top 10% about 40% D1, about 12% D7, about 4% D30 — [GameDevReports summary of GameAnalytics 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game)
- GameAnalytics Q1 2024: medians D1 22.91%, D7 4.20%, D28 0.85%. Puzzle has the "highest D7 retention in Casual category across all regions" — [GameDevReports Q1'24](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-benchmarks-in-mobile)
- Unverified genre table: casual puzzle 30-40% D1, 10-20% D7, 3-7% D30 (no traceable source) — [Juego Studio](https://www.juegostudio.com/blog/how-to-increase-user-retention-and-increase-your-games-lifetime)

### Inferences
- For Lapdle: protect the 7-day window (a streak milestone at day 3/7, a gentle "streak at risk" push in the evening only for opted-in players) and add forgiveness (one auto freeze per week, or repair after outages). Duolingo's evidence suggests lifts of a few % per mechanic, not step changes.
- Ask for push only after the first completed game/share, with an in-page pre-prompt explaining "one reminder a day". This avoids the quiet-UI penalty and the 12% desktop baseline.
- Leaderboards/championship: no hard public data found for daily web games. The monthly reset logic (fresh chance for newcomers) is plausible but untested here. With 10-20 DAU, sparse boards can demotivate, so show "you vs friends/today's average" rather than an empty global ranking.
- "One per day" scarcity plus an unlimited/archive mode is the common pattern among successful dle sites (Wordle Unlimited's search success shows demand for more plays). The archive helps SEO and binge play while the daily stays the habit anchor.
- Mobile D1 ~22% / D7 ~4% are rough reference points. Daily puzzle web games with a habit loop plausibly beat D7 medians, but no web-specific benchmark was found.

### Gaps
- No public D1/D7/D30 data for web daily games (Wordle/NYT never published cohort retention).
- No data on leaderboard or named-ranking effects, monthly resets, or archive effects for daily games specifically.
- No primary Duolingo stat on streak freeze's churn impact (the 21% figure is unverified).
- No measured effect of daily web push reminders on return rate for games.

## 4. What harms growth

### Takeaway
Direct evidence is thin. The best-supported harms are asking for notification permission too early (Chrome penalizes it), spoiling answers in shares (the Wordle community norm against it), and novelty decay. Claims about sign-up walls, slow loads and intrusive ads for daily games are mostly opinion in this research.

### Cited Findings
- Early or aggressive notification prompts get very low acceptance, and Chrome demotes such sites to quiet UI — [Chromium blog](https://blog.chromium.org/2020/01/introducing-quieter-permission-ui-for.html)
- Spoiler-free norms kept Wordle's sharing community welcoming. Posting answers would have broken the loop — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/stefficao/how-wordle-went-viral-strategy)
- Clone traffic decays (wordle-game.co down about 39% per Similarweb). Even Wordle plays fell from 5.3B to 4.2B between 2024 and 2025 — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/wordle-game.co/); [A Media Operator](https://www.amediaoperator.com/analysis/wordle-tv-show-new-york-times-games-jonathan-knight/)
- Market saturation: 399 Wordle spinoffs listed in one directory and 318 daily games in another — [Wordly](https://wordly.org/wordle-games); [Relaydle](https://relaydle.com/)
- Very hard puzzles create mass streak loss (5.6M streaks on one day), which can generate attention or frustration — [TODAY via AOL](https://www.aol.com/exclusive-wordle-puzzle-ended-5-013624721.html)
- Wordle originally had no account, no ads and a single puzzle per day. Its simplicity is widely credited (commentary) — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/stefficao/how-wordle-went-viral-strategy)

### Inferences
- Lapdle's anonymous, no-sign-up design matches the Wordle template. Keep nicknames optional for leaderboards rather than mandatory.
- Spreading across too many modes risks diluting the daily ritual and the shared puzzle that makes comparison work. NYT added new games (Connections, Strands, Pips) as separate products rather than variants of Wordle.

### Gaps
- No measured data found on sign-up walls, page speed or ad intrusiveness for daily web games specifically. General web CWV/conversion studies were not researched here.
- No postmortems from failed dle creators with numbers were found (Reddit/Indie Hackers searches returned nothing usable).
